Kairo Elite Section 1 — Dashboard, Notes & History
01
Dashboard, Notes & History
Your daily command centre, the smart notepad and the complete archive of everything you have completed — the first and last menus of the sidebar.
🏠
Dashboard — Command Centre
Free

The main screen of the app. A personalised greeting based on the time of day, status cards (overdue tasks, important tasks, habits completed, tasks today), the monthly mini calendar and the Executive Productivity panel.

The Executive Productivity panel shows the day's percentage, the comparison with yesterday, the weekly rate and the 7-day trend — all at a glance.

1
Open the app — Dashboard is the first menu in the sidebar
2
Check the state of your day in the four cards at the top
3
Track productivity in the Executive panel below
📝
Notes — with Search and Voice Dictation
Free

An integrated notepad with a side list, instant search by title and content, and an editor with title + body. All notes sync automatically with your account.

The "Dictate" button uses your microphone to turn speech into text — perfect for capturing ideas on the move, between meetings or while driving.

1
Go to Notes → "+ New note"
2
Type or click "Dictate" to convert speech to text
3
Use the search at the top to find any note in seconds
🗄️
History — Analysis and Export
Pro

A complete archive of all completed tasks, with filters by period (start and end date), category and status. Ideal for reviewing what was done in a week, month or quarter.

Records can be exported for external analysis — reports, invoicing or performance reviews.

1
Go to History in the sidebar
2
Set the period, category and status with the filters at the top
3
Export the filtered records for external use
Kairo Elite Section 2 — Task Management
02
Task Management
The core of the platform. Create, organise and track all your daily tasks with priority, time, category and more — all synced in real time.
📋
Daily Tasks
Free

Create tasks with a title, start time, priority (High / Medium / Low) and category. Tasks appear sorted by time and are marked as complete with one click.

Overdue tasks are automatically highlighted in the "Overdue" section so you never lose track of anything important.

1
Click "+ New Task" in the Tasks panel
2
Fill in title, time, priority and category
3
Click the circle to mark it as complete — it appears in History
🔄
Recurring Tasks
Free

Tasks that renew automatically every day, week, month, or on specific days chosen through an interactive calendar.

At the end of the day, the recurring task is automatically recreated for the next period — with no user intervention.

1
When creating or editing a task, enable "Recurring"
2
Choose: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Specific Days
3
For specific days, open the calendar and select the days of the week
📅
Visual Agenda (Mini Calendar)
Free

An integrated monthly calendar showing which days have scheduled tasks. Click any day to see a popup with all tasks for that day — perfect for planning your week or month.

1
In the main panel, see the mini calendar below Overdue Tasks
2
Days with coloured dots have scheduled tasks
3
Click a day to see the popup with all its tasks
⏱️
Focus Mode — Built-in Pomodoro
Pro

A Pomodoro timer directly inside each task. Work in 25-minute blocks of total focus, followed by 5-minute breaks — without leaving the app.

The number of completed pomodoros is recorded on each task, letting you measure how much real time was invested.

1
Open a task and click the ⏱ Focus icon
2
The 25-min timer starts — with a sound notification at the end
3
Automatic 5-min break, then a new cycle
Kairo Elite Section 3 — Habits & Goals
03
Habits & Goals
Build solid routines and track long-term goals with full visibility over your progress — day after day.
✅
Daily & Weekly Habits
Free

Create habits with a daily or weekly frequency. Each day, active habits appear to be validated — yes or no. Kairo automatically calculates your streak (consecutive days) and the weekly completion rate.

Weekly habits run from Monday to Sunday: if you add a habit on Wednesday, it appears every day until Sunday so you can validate each day. The following week, it starts again automatically.

1
Go to Habits and click "+ New Habit"
2
Set the name, icon and frequency (Daily / Weekly)
3
Every day, click ✓ to validate — your streak grows
4
Click the pencil ✏️ to edit an existing habit
🎯
30/90-Day Goals
Pro

Set major 30 or 90-day objectives, broken down into actionable sub-tasks. The progress bar updates automatically as sub-tasks are completed.

Perfect for OKRs, product launches, campaigns or personal transformations — all visible in one place.

1
Go to Goals → "+ New Goal"
2
Set the title, deadline (30 or 90 days) and add sub-tasks
3
Complete sub-tasks to advance the progress bar
💡 Why habits and goals change everything
Tasks solve today. Habits build who you are tomorrow. Goals ensure today's work has a clear direction. Kairo is the only platform that unites all three in an integrated view of personal and professional progress.
Kairo Elite Section 4 — Team Mode
04
Team Mode
Create your team inside Kairo, delegate tasks with deadlines, track each member's progress and keep everyone accountable — in real time.
👥
Create & Manage Team
Pro

Create a team with a unique 8-character code. Invite members by email — Kairo automatically sends an invitation email with the code and instructions to join.

1
Go to Team → "Create Team", set the name
2
Click "Invite Member", enter the email → the invitation is sent automatically
3
The member joins with the code in "Join a Team"
↗️
Delegation with Accountability
Pro

Delegate tasks to any member with a title, explanatory note, exact deadline (calendar + hour/minute) and priority. The member sees the delegated task in real time on their team panel.

The accountability panel shows as a percentage how many tasks each member completed this week — total visibility for the leader.

1
In Team → "+ Delegate Task"
2
Select the member, set the deadline on the calendar and choose hour/minute
3
The member marks the task as complete — it appears in the real-time feed
📡
Real-Time Activity Feed
Pro

A live feed showing what the whole team is doing — who completed what, who delegated to whom, and who just joined the team. It updates instantly without reloading the page.

Each entry shows the member's avatar with initials, the colour-coded action (green = completed, blue = delegated) and the elapsed time ("5 min ago", "2h ago").

"The problem with teams is not a lack of talent — it's a lack of visibility. Kairo solves that in real time, without unnecessary status meetings."

Kairo Elite Section 5 — Kairo AI & Decision Journal
05
Kairo AI & Decision Journal
Features exclusive to leaders — an AI chat with the real context of your agenda and a journal to record, review and learn from every strategic decision.
🧠
Kairo AI Chat — The context of your agenda
CEO / Pro

Kairo AI Chat answers based on the real context of your agenda, tasks, habits and progress. You can ask what you should prioritise, which tasks are overdue, or how your productivity is doing.

Answers are generated from your real data — not generic responses, but concrete advice about your day.

1
In the app, click the floating Kairo AI button in the bottom-right corner
2
With Pro, open the chat and type a question about your day
3
Get a contextual answer based on your tasks, habits and progress
⚖️
Decision Journal
CEO / Pro

Record every strategic decision with context, reasoning and a confidence level (1 to 5). Set a review deadline — when it arrives, Kairo alerts you to open it and evaluate the outcome (0 to 10) and what you learned.

Over time, the Decision Journal becomes a CEO's most valuable asset: an auditable record of every decision, outcome and lesson — something no other productivity app offers.

1
Go to Decisions in the sidebar → "+ New Decision"
2
Fill in the decision, context, category, confidence level and review deadline
3
When the deadline arrives, Kairo alerts you — click "Review" and rate 0-10
4
The dashboard shows the success rate of your decisions over time
📊
Confidence Gauge
Each decision has a coloured circular indicator (red/yellow/green) that visually shows the confidence level at the moment of the decision.
🏷️
Decision Categories
Strategy, People, Finance, Operations, Product or Personal — classify each decision to analyse patterns by area.
⚠️
Review Alerts
Kairo alerts you when a decision has reached its review deadline and has not yet been evaluated — you never lose track.
📈
Success Rate
The percentage of decisions with an outcome ≥ 6/10. Track how the quality of your decisions evolves over time.
Kairo Elite Section 6 — Notifications & Email
06
Notifications & Communication
Kairo brings you back when it matters — with smart push notifications, a daily email with the day's tasks, and a weekly productivity summary.
🔔
Push Notifications
Free

Receive notifications on your phone or computer X minutes before a task with a set time. Never miss a meeting, deadline or important commitment again.

Works even with the browser closed — the service worker ensures notification delivery.

1
Click the 🔔 icon in the top bar → "Enable Notifications"
2
Allow notifications in the browser when prompted
3
Set how many minutes before each task you want to be notified
📧
Automatic Daily Email
Free

Receive a daily email, at the time you choose, with all the day's tasks — so you never start the day without knowing what lies ahead. The email is sent automatically via the Resend platform, with no intervention.

1
Click the avatar (top-right corner) → "Daily Email"
2
Enter your email and choose the delivery time (e.g. 08:00)
3
Every day at that time, you receive the task summary
📊
Weekly Summary (Weekly Review)
Pro

Every Sunday, automatically receive an email with the week's summary: completed tasks, current streak, productivity rate compared with the previous week, and the habits you kept.

The format is clean and executive — you can forward it directly to your board or team as a weekly report.

1
Click the avatar → "Weekly Summary"
2
Enter the destination email and enable automatic delivery
3
Receive your weekly productivity report every Sunday
