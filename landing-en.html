FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is Kairo free?

Yes. The free plan includes tasks, habits and the dashboard, forever. Pro (€9.99/month) unlocks Kairo AI, full sync and analytics history.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Sign up with Google or email and start using it immediately. No card, no commitment.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest via Firebase (Google Cloud), with secure authentication. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from your profile.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Kairo is a responsive web app that works on any device — desktop, tablet and phone — and keeps working offline.

Can I use it with my team?

Yes. With Pro you can create teams, assign tasks, track activity and share notes with your members.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime, with no penalties. Your account stays active on the free plan and all your data remains intact.
